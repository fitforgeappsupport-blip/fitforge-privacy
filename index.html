FitForge Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 16, 2026

The short version: FitForge stores all of your data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal information. Period.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

FitForge does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. We do not have user accounts, cloud storage, analytics services, or advertising SDKs. The following data stays entirely on your device:

  • Workout logs, sets, reps, and weight data
  • Nutrition logs, food entries, and macro tracking data
  • Body measurements and weight history
  • Progress photos
  • Training blocks and templates
  • App settings and preferences
  • Custom foods you create
  • Daily readiness check-ins

2. Data Stored on Your Device

All app data is stored in a local SQLite database and local storage on your phone. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to uninstall the app or clear its data through your device settings.

Progress photos are saved to the app's private storage directory on your device. They are not uploaded anywhere.

3. Third-Party Services

FitForge connects to the following external services solely to look up food nutrition data when you search for or scan a food item:

  • USDA FoodData Central API (fdc.nal.usda.gov) - A free, public U.S. government database. FitForge sends only the food search query or barcode. No personal data is transmitted.
  • Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org) - A free, open-source food database. FitForge sends only the food search query or barcode. No personal data is transmitted.
  • Google Gemini API (optional) - If you choose to use the AI meal plan or meal discovery features, your nutrition goals and food preferences are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate meal suggestions. This requires you to provide your own API key. No workout data, body measurements, photos, or other personal information is sent. You can use FitForge without this feature.

FitForge does not use any analytics, crash reporting, or advertising services.

4. Camera and Barcode Scanner

FitForge requests camera access for two purposes:

  • Barcode scanning - to look up nutrition information for packaged foods
  • Progress photos - to take and store body progress photos locally on your device

Camera data is processed entirely on your device. No images or camera data are transmitted to any server.

5. Health Connect (Samsung Health)

FitForge can optionally connect to Android Health Connect to write completed workout data to Samsung Health or other compatible health apps. This connection is initiated by you, requires your explicit permission, and data flows only from FitForge to Health Connect on your device. FitForge does not read health data from Health Connect.

6. Children's Privacy

FitForge is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

7. Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it at all times. To delete all FitForge data:

  • Uninstall the app, or
  • Go to your device Settings > Apps > FitForge > Storage > Clear Data

8. Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, the changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Since FitForge does not collect email addresses or contact information, we cannot notify you directly. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
